|---|---|

## Three-sentence elevator pitch

Across 2,123 Norwegian youth track-and-field athletes followed for up to 14 years, baseline-window competition volume (ages 13–14) alone classified eventual senior retention with cross-validated AUC = 0.751 (OR = 2.40 per SD). The signal decomposed into two complementary patterns: athletes already differed in volume at age 14 (OR = 2.79) and an additional within-athlete pull-back across ages 14–15 (OR = 2.44) doubled the model's pseudo-R². Findings replicated across two independent birth-year cohorts and support a tractable retention strategy: register-based behavioral surveillance from end of age 14 (PPV ≈ 0.94 at moderate threshold).
