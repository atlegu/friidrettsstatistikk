## Three-sentence elevator pitch

Across 2,123 Norwegian youth track-and-field athletes followed for up to 14 years, competition volume at age 15–16 was a vastly stronger predictor of senior retention than performance level, more than tripling Cox model discrimination (C 0.57 → 0.85). Future dropouts visibly reduced participation 2–3 years before formal exit, quantifying the qualitative observation (Espedalen, 2025; Eliasson & Johansson, 2021) that dropout is a gradual behavioral process rather than a single decision. The pattern replicates across two independent birth-year cohorts and supports a tractable retention strategy: register-based behavioral surveillance.
