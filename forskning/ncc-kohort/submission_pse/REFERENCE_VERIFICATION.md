## Corrected in this pass

1. **Espedalen & Seippel 2024** (§1.1, §4.2): "lower-income families" → "minority youth from less-resourced families" (abstract: *minority youths with less resources* highlighted sport expenses; gender/ethnicity entangled).
2. **Worley & Smith 2026 / Zhong 2026** (§1.2): both are cross-sectional (path analysis; HLM), not longitudinal → "short longitudinal designs" reworded to "studies of proximal psychological constructs such as burnout, motivation, and peer relations".
3. **Back 2022** (§1.2, S-M2 unchanged): meta-analysis covers *team sport* → "adolescent team-sport dropout".
4. **Enoksen scope** (§4.2): "the only long-term Norwegian follow-up" → "the 25-year Norwegian follow-up" (Lippe 1976 and Næsje 1985 exist, per Enoksen's own review).
5. **Scanlan 2016** (§1.1): "predicts exactly such divergent responses" softened to "gives such divergent responses a theoretical basis" (theoretical inference, not an SCQ-2 result).
6. **Baseline-meet mechanics** (§2.2, per Lerøy-lekene regulations): entry is by *district federations under district quotas* (not "through their club"), no qualifying performance standard, **no entry fee**; "soft selection … design feature" restored.
7. **Venue coverage** (§2.2, §4.9): the third venue is *Midt- og Nord-Norge* — northern districts are served by the mid-Norway venue; "northern Norway is not represented" removed.
8. **Bakken vs Espedalen attribution** (§1.1, §1.3 H1, §4.4): Bakken 2019 measures participation and *training investment* variation (4 of 10 train ≥20×/month; quitters train half as often) but contains **no ambition/motive data** (p. 23 disclaims it) → investment variation attributed to Bakken; heavy/light *motive* stratification and quit-when-demanding mechanism attributed to Espedalen 2025; H1's citation changed to (Espedalen, 2025; Scanlan et al., 2016).
