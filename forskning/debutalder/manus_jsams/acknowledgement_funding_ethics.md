# Acknowledgement, funding information and confirmation of ethical compliance

**Manuscript:** Does starting age predict who stays? Competition debut and long-term participation in youth track and field

**Author:** Atle G. Guttormsen, School of Economics and Business, Norwegian University of Life Sciences (NMBU), Ås, Norway

## Acknowledgement

The author thanks the Norwegian Athletics Federation, whose systematic registration of competition results makes population research of this kind possible.

## Funding

This research did not receive any specific grant from funding agencies in the public, commercial, or not-for-profit sectors. No external financial support was received, and no funding organisation had any role in the design of the study, the collection, analysis and interpretation of the data, the writing of the manuscript, or the decision to submit it for publication.

## Confirmation of ethical compliance

The study analyses competition results registered by the Norwegian Athletics Federation. Results from age 13 are publicly listed; records for the 10–12 age classes are registered but not publicly released by the federation, and for those ages only participation itself (whether and when an athlete competed) was used. No performance data were used at any age.

The study is not medical or health research as defined by Norway's Health Research Act (Helseforskningsloven §§ 2 and 4), which governs approval by the Regional Committees for Medical and Health Research Ethics, so ethics committee approval was not required. Sikt (the Norwegian Agency for Shared Services in Education and Research) confirmed that the use of the data raises no data-protection concerns. No participants were contacted or recruited, and informed consent was not required. No individual athlete is identified in the manuscript or the supplementary material. The work was carried out in accordance with the principles of the Declaration of Helsinki as they apply to register-based research without contact with participants.

The manuscript has not been published previously, is not under consideration elsewhere, and is not available on a preprint server.
